# Open Intelligence Stack (OIS)

**Open Intelligence Stack (OIS)** is a modular, governed AI architecture specification designed to build transparent, auditable, and human-supervised intelligence systems. It decomposes AI capabilities into specialized modules that function like a professional engagement team, each with defined responsibilities, legal review obligations, evidence requirements, and human-in-the-loop approval gates.

**Nothing Hidden. Everything Traceable.**

---

# Core Design Principles

- Modular intelligence through specialized modules
- Human-in-the-loop governance for all critical decisions
- Continuous legal analysis across all modules
- Evidence-based reasoning with scoring and validation
- Full provenance tracking for every artifact
- Immutable auditability and traceability
- License-aware and compliance-first architecture
- Replaceable and extensible module system
- Deterministic workflow orchestration where possible

---

# System Architecture

OIS is structured as a **multi-module intelligence stack**, where each module operates as an independent governed service.

## Core Modules

1. Executive Orchestrator  
2. Requirements & Discovery  
3. Research & Evidence  
4. Strategy & Planning  
5. Financial Analysis  
6. Technical Architecture  
7. Implementation Engine  
8. Risk, Ethics & Governance  
9. Validation & Quality Assurance  
10. Executive Decision Support  

Each module contains:

- Defined mission scope
- Input/output contracts
- Authority boundaries
- Legal analysis responsibilities
- Risk evaluation requirements
- Human approval checkpoints
- Audit logging requirements

# Design Philosophy

Open Intelligence Stack treats AI not as a monolithic model, but as a **governed intelligence organization** where each module is a specialized professional role operating under strict evidence, legal, and human oversight constraints. The system is designed for environments where trust, accountability, and compliance are as important as intelligence capability.
