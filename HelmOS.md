# HelmOS

**Progress You Can Audit.**

HelmOS is a modular, local-first operating layer that wraps existing AI agents with governance, intent verification, trust management, memory, auditing, evaluation, workflow orchestration, incident management, and controlled self-improvement.

Rather than replacing existing AI agents, HelmOS acts as a universal wrapper that adds human oversight, constitutional governance, explainability, accountability, and measurable improvement to any compatible agent framework.

## Core Principles

- Human Intent. Verified Execution.
- Human authority remains supreme.
- Every action must be auditable.
- Every decision must be explainable.
- Every improvement must be measurable.
- Every execution must remain accountable.
- Local-first and self-hosted by default.
- Modular and extensible architecture.
- Open governance through AGPL-3.0+ licensing.
