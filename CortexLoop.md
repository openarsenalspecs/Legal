# CortexLoop

**Transparent intelligence, enforced by design.**

CortexLoop is a **human-in-the-loop AI orchestration system** designed to ensure that all AI-assisted reasoning, planning, and workflow execution remains **fully transparent, auditable, and human-governed at every step**.

It is not an autonomous agent.  
It is a **structured cognition and decision-loop system** that amplifies human decision-making while enforcing constraints, traceability, and governance rules.

---

## Core Philosophy

CortexLoop is built on three principles:

- **Human authority is absolute**
- **All reasoning must be visible and structured**
- **No system action occurs outside an enforced loop**

---

## 🧠 System Overview

CortexLoop transforms AI from a reactive assistant into a **structured reasoning engine inside a controlled decision loop**.

Instead of producing single opaque outputs, it:
- Generates multiple structured options
- Evaluates tradeoffs and risks
- Routes decisions back to the human
- Logs all reasoning and outcomes

---

## 🔁 Core Human-in-the-Loop System

### Loop Cycle

1. Input is received  
2. Intent is parsed and structured  
3. Context is retrieved and analyzed  
4. Multiple solution paths are generated  
5. Risk and confidence are evaluated  
6. Options are presented to the human  
7. Human selects or modifies direction  
8. Approved actions are executed  
9. Decision is logged  
10. Loop repeats  

---

## 🧠 Core Features

### 1. Reasoning Engine
- Structured interpretation of user intent  
- Multi-step decomposition of tasks  
- Tradeoff analysis across multiple solution paths  
- Assumption detection and clarification  

---

### 2. Multi-Track Reasoning System
CortexLoop generates multiple parallel approaches:

- Optimized solution path  
- Conservative safe path  
- Minimal lightweight path  
- Experimental or exploratory path  

---

### 3. Confidence & Risk Layer
- 🟢 Low risk → proceed with minimal interruption  
- 🟡 Medium risk → present + recommend  
- 🔴 High risk → require explicit human confirmation  

This layer controls **attention routing only**, not execution.

---

### 4. Intent Stabilization Layer
- Detects scope changes mid-task  
- Identifies contradictory instructions  
- Requests confirmation before shifting system direction  

---

### 5. Policy Engine (Constraint Enforcement)
- Enforces system-wide rules  
- Blocks invalid outputs before rendering  
- Ensures AGPL compliance and attribution rules  
- Enforces structural and formatting constraints  

---

### 6. Decision Ledger (Audit System)
- Records AI outputs and human decisions  
- Tracks full reasoning history  
- Maintains structured logs for traceability  

---

### 7. Replay Engine
- Reconstructs full decision flows  
- Replays reasoning steps deterministically  
- Enables debugging and audit verification  

---

### 8. Constraint Memory System
- Persistent behavioral rules  
- Enforced formatting and structural preferences  
- System-wide rule consistency across sessions  

---

### 9. Explanation & Decision Trace Layer
- Explains why decisions were made  
- Shows rejected alternatives  
- Documents assumptions and tradeoffs  

---

### 10. Context Graph System
- Connects projects, decisions, and workflows  
- Maintains cross-project awareness  
- Enables structured long-term reasoning  

---

### 11. Loop Controller (Core Orchestrator)
- Manages HITL cycle execution  
- Enforces checkpoints  
- Prevents silent or autonomous execution  

---

### 12. Execution Safety Model
- No irreversible actions without approval  
- Human override always available  
- No hidden state changes  
- All outputs must pass policy validation  

---

## 🧬 System Guarantees

CortexLoop guarantees:

- Human is always the final authority  
- No autonomous execution paths exist  
- All outputs are traceable and auditable  
- All policy rules are enforced before output  
- All decisions are logged in structured format
